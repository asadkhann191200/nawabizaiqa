✦   Our Heritage   ✦

A Legacy of Royal Flavours

Born from the grandeur of the Mughal courts, Nawabi Zaiqa brings you a feast worthy of emperors. Our recipes are passed down through generations — slow-cooked meats, hand-ground spice blends, and techniques perfected over centuries of royal kitchens. Every bite carries the soul of Delhi's old bazaars, the richness of saffron-laced gravies, and the warmth of charcoal-smoked kebabs. We don't merely serve food; we serve an experience of Mughlai royalty.

✦   Our Signature Dish   ✦

Nahari
Kulcha

Rising with the Fajr azaan in Old Delhi, Nihari has fed nawabs and labourers alike for over three centuries. Our version simmers overnight — bone marrow, ginger, and nineteen whole spices — until the gravy becomes liquid silk. Paired with hand-pulled kulcha from our tandoor. There is no dish more honest, more royal, more Nawabi.
